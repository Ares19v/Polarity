### ⏱️ Quick Start-Up Routine for Polarity:

#### **Step 1: Boot your Android Emulator / Connect Device**
1. Open **Android Studio** and launch your virtual device via Device Manager (or connect a physical device via USB debugging).

#### **Step 2: Start the Server & Load on Phone**
1. Open your terminal in the `Polarity` project directory.
2. Run:
   ```bash
   npm run android
   ```
   *Expo will boot the Metro server, connect to your Android device/emulator, and launch the native Polarity app!*

#### **Step 3: Web Preview (Optional)**
1. Open your browser to `http://localhost:8081` to view or debug the bundle.
