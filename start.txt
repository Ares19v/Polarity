### ⏱️ Your 30-Second Start-Up Routine for Polarity:

#### **Step 1: Boot your Virtual Phone**
1. Open **Android Studio** on your laptop.
2. Open the **Device Manager** (Virtual Device Manager).
3. Click the green **"Play" (Launch)** button next to your virtual phone to boot it up! (Wait 10 seconds for it to show the home screen).

#### **Step 2: Start the Server & Load on Phone**
1. Open your terminal/IDE in your project folder (`C:\Users\Devansh Tyagi\Desktop\Projects\Polarity`).
2. Run this single command:
   ```
   npm run android
   ```
   *Expo will boot up the project code server, detect your running virtual phone, and immediately launch the native Polarity app inside it!*

#### **Step 3: Open your Browser Preview**
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to:
   ```
   http://localhost:8081
   ```
